# Milestone 1: First real battle

**Done-gate:** "In a browser, I trade shots with an enemy tank in turns (move, aim, set power, fire, wind matters), one of us dies or falls off the map, a You Win / You Lose screen appears, and R starts a fresh battle on new terrain."

**Status:** passes in headless Chromium at 1280×720 with real key presses. You haven't tried it yet.

- QA round 1 lost 5 of 5 battles: the enemy hit on its first shot, its HP bar couldn't be read, tanks and shells were tiny, and restarting could leave an old shell landing in the new battle. All of that was fixed.
- QA round 2 won 1 of 5 by hand (YOU WIN reached, R gave new terrain with full HP), with no console errors. The review then measured the enemy hitting directly 92–95% of the time once its aim settled, so its misses were changed to land to the side, wind was cut to −6..6, and the dotted aim paths were made bigger. Nobody has played a full battle by hand since that last tuning. A final browser smoke test (aim, fire, enemy reply) showed both shots missing and no errors.
- Not seen in any run: a tank falling off the map. The rule is coded and unit-tested, but no browser battle produced it.
- Tests at the head: `tests/run.sh` gives 55 passed, 0 failed in about 30 s. That includes 10 seeded AI-vs-AI battles, which all end in 13–22 turns. GitHub Actions runs the same tests on every push, then exports the Web build to gh-pages.

**Scorecard so far:** [`docs/chief/scorecard.md`](../chief/scorecard.md). First-pass reviews 0/5, merged/planned 5/5 (stuck 0). Escaped bugs and guesses kept get filled in after you play.

## How to try it
1. Switch on GitHub Pages once (the steps are in the PR).
2. Open https://thomas-tahk.github.io/rogue-artillery/m1-first-battle/ and click the game once so it gets the keyboard.
3. ←/→ drive (fuel-limited) · ↑/↓ angle · W/S power · Space fire · R new battle.

Watch for:
- The enemy's first shots should miss by a lot and then close in on you. If it still feels unfair or too easy, say so.
- The green dotted line is your last shot and the orange one is the enemy's. Use them to correct your aim.
- Craters: shooting the ground under a tank can drop it into a hole, or off the map.

## Decisions that need you (most important first)
1. **Enemy difficulty.** Its misses now land to the side, starting about 180 px away and closing in ×0.8 per shot to at least 45 px. Tell me if it's too hard or too easy.
2. **Power with W/S keys, not a timing bar.** This is how I read the GDD's "no timing-based power". The alternative is fixed power per shot type, so angle alone sets range.
3. **Dotted line showing each side's last shot.** It's an aiming aid that isn't in the GDD. Keep it or remove it.
4. **Controls:** ←/→ drive, ↑/↓ angle, W/S power. Mouse click-drag aiming (from the GDD) is planned for M2.
5. **Next milestone:** I'd do M2 "Battle feel" (explosions, screen shake, a camera that follows the shell, mouse aiming) before M3 "Run skeleton". Say if you'd rather have the run structure first.
