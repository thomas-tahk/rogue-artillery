## Best 3 CC0 bases for a full game
1. Kenney Tanks: only side-view-compatible, coherent tank kit with explosions; weak on personality.
2. Kenney Background Elements Redux + Particle Pack: scenery and FX textures, consistent flat style.
3. Seamless HD landscape in parts: tileable parallax silhouettes that recolour well with a gradient map.
Alternate: Kenney UI Pack for the HUD.
